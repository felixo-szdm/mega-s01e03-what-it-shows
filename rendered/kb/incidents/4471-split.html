parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. Day and month come out swapped: “Booked for 2 December. The invite says 12 February.”

hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type month first as told, and the form reads the date day first, the tenant’s way. Same swap, different bug: the fix is in the form, not the parser.

one-day: a booking moves by one day, or shows a different day in Lisbon and Toronto. Nothing is swapped. That is a timezone or daylight-saving conversion. The desk spotted these (desk notes) and counts them under parser by tenant, so they sit in that row too; a parser fix will not close them.

How they ended up in one ticket

  • Merging by subject (decision 0001). All three problems produce the same subjects: “Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”. Matching subjects merged them without anyone reading a body.
  • Intake tags are a guess from the reporter’s browser. Every report tagged hint came from an en-US browser, whatever its tenant or form. The tag is no guide to the cause.
  • The export repeats rows. Its pages overlap, so count by report id.

For the desk

  • Group by tenant, form and channel (causes.ts), not by subject.
  • Read a sample of each group’s bodies before you call a big ticket one issue.
  • A decision record is never edited; a new one should replace 0001.
